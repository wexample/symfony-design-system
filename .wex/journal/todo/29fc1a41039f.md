# Header responsive: what an application puts in the header on a phone

Opened: 2026-10-07
Updated: 2026-10-07
Author: agent:main

Asked by weeger: « pour le responsive du header on verra plus tard ».

## Context

Phase 5 (layout, commit `f7e24023`) made the dashboard header hold on a phone for what the
design system draws itself: below `m` the controls of `.header--menu` keep their icon and
give up their word (`.button-menu--label` visually hidden, caret gone), and the title is cut
short (`.header--body > .header--title`, ellipsis, `min-width: 0`).

What an application adds to the header is left as it is. Seen in the Mojoe design system
(`MOJOE/local/design-system/front/layouts/private/layout.html.twig`, another agent's work):
a search box in `header_body` (`.header--search`, `width: 100%`, `max-width: 24rem`) takes
the whole header body at 400px and the page title, which now gives way, collapses to nothing.

## To decide, then build

- What the header body may hold beside the title on a phone: a search folded to its icon
  (opening over the header, or into the page), the title hidden behind it, or the search
  moved out of the header below `m`.
- Whether the design system offers a slot for it (`header_search`?) so the fold is the
  design system's, instead of each application styling its own.
- Width classes: `css/mixins/_window.scss` (`$window-m`, `$window-l`), the loader's sizes.

## Not this

- The workspace rows (tree + page + side zone) on a phone: they scroll sideways, a degraded
  version that works; weeger: improve only if a need shows.
